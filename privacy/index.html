Our privacy promise

Effective August 8, 2026

SimplyFi builds financial tools that are simple, affordable, private, and honest. This page explains the promises behind those words.

The short version: we do not sell your personal data, show ads, or use your financial life to target you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade personal data.
  • We don't run advertising or ad tracking.
  • We don't require an account just to use a SimplyFi tool.
  • We don't access financial information beyond what you choose to provide for a feature.

Budget

Budget is currently being developed. Before it is released, we will publish a complete, plain-language policy describing exactly what information the product uses, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

Our intended approach is data minimization: collect only what is essential for the tool to work, keep it under your control, and never use it for advertising.

Questions

If you have a question about this promise or a future SimplyFi product, contact us at hello@simplyfi.app.
